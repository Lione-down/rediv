## Product contract

1. The latest official authenticated LibTV Canvas is the sole final parity authority. Third-party reviews, retained screenshots, public marketing pages, and prior T5-094 evidence may guide discovery but cannot close a scenario when the official Canvas can be observed directly.
2. Parity is assessed independently on three axes:
   - visual effect: hierarchy, geometry, spacing, typography metrics, node/card/media shape, overlays, edges, states, and motion;
   - functional effect: the user-visible result, state transitions, graph mutation, persistence, history, recovery, and error outcome;
   - interaction effect: entry placement, gesture, action order, progressive disclosure, focus, dismissal, and feedback.
3. A scenario is `MATCHED` only when all three axes pass. Control presence, a similar feature name, or Revid-only regression evidence is insufficient.
4. Revid keeps its graphite/violet semantic color system, branding, copy, provider/model ownership, and lawful icons/assets. LibTV private source, prompts, fonts, icons, logos, credentials, and visual assets are never copied.
5. A Revid feature with no LibTV counterpart remains visible, reachable, and functional. It is classified as `EXTENSION_PRESERVED`, not removed, hidden, fabricated, or disabled merely to improve screenshot similarity.
6. Revid extensions must not move, resize, or alter the action path of the LibTV-equivalent core region. When a spatial conflict exists, use a clearly measured adjacent extension slot or section that remains one level reachable.
7. Existing Revid auth, project, Canvas, typed graph, command/history, autosave, asset, quota, task, provider, result, Storyboard, Graph Access, revision, Skill, and mobile review-only boundaries remain authoritative.
8. Opening a menu, toolbar, composer, resource selector, model selector, optimization surface, operation panel, Inspector, or extension entry must not mutate graph, history, revision, or autosave state. Mutation begins only after the corresponding explicit confirmed action.
9. Every visible actionable control must work or be explicitly disabled with an accessible reason. Inert cost, optimization, model, provider, attachment, Send, or recovery controls are release blockers.
10. T5-094 documented deferrals do not count as T5-096 parity proof. Every in-scope official Canvas behavior must be newly observed and closed or T5-096 remains `VERIFYING`/`BLOCKED`.
11. Human Owner scope decision on 2026-07-20: Revid does not implement LibTV Director/`导演台`, does not show a Director entry, and does not ship an inert or disabled imitation. Official Director evidence remains observation context only and is `HUMAN_EXCLUDED_NOT_APPLICABLE` for T5-096 parity counters.
